Privacy and retention

  • Kept videos are stored privately on this server, are never public, and cannot be opened by address alone.
  • Each kept video is deleted automatically 7 days after upload, or sooner if you delete it. Deleting a video keeps its reports, but their playback links stop working.
  • Reports stay in your history until you delete them. Nothing deletes them automatically. You can delete one report, or all reports after a confirmation.
  • Frames sampled from a clip are sent to the Claude API for review. Only the frames, the frame times, and your notes are sent. Anthropic's API data terms apply to them.
  • Live camera sessions send frames only and are not stored as video.
